# Evidence-led service delivery — shared Phase 1 process

Research basis: Blackpine 30-Service Expansion Research (20 Nov 2026), pp. 14–19.

| Stage | Mandatory output | Go/no-go |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Scope | signed authority, client owner, specific assets/sites, prohibited actions, test window | no testing before authorization |
| 2. Discover | inventory, business owner, asset state, date | unowned/unknown assets excluded |
| 3. Verify | reproducible, appropriately redacted evidence; human review | scanner output alone not enough |
| 4. Prioritize | impact, affected business process, owner, fix order | severity defensible |
| 5. Implement | approved change request, recovery plan and rollback | no uncontrolled production change |
| 6. Retest | before/after proof, residual risk, reviewer sign-off | never close solely because a checkbox changed |
| 7. Maintain | bounded follow-up cadence, retention/deletion schedule | recurring activity only if contracted |

## Evidence status

UNVERIFIED → VALIDATED → RETESTED; FALSE_POSITIVE is a separate terminal disposition. Every observation records scope, collector, timestamp, provenance, authorized method and review state. Keep raw restricted evidence out of public code and ordinary logs.

## Report structure

1. Engagement ID, client, version, confidential marking and authorization.
2. Scope, exclusions, dates, limitations and methodology.
3. Executive summary with observed risks, not invented numerical scores.
4. Assets and verified findings with evidence references.
5. Risk impact, prioritized remediation owner, rollback/change dependencies.
6. Retest results, unresolved risks, client decision log.
7. Evidence retention, deletion, review signatures and next verification date.

## Launch readiness

PROPOSED → DEFINED → IMPLEMENTED → PILOT_VERIFIED → APPROVED. Publication only after APPROVED and separately checked licensing, competency, insurance, partner responsibility and operating hours where applicable. These are internal milestones, not standards certification.
