## Required evidence before changing OPEN
- Written determination with service-specific scope, regulator/legal reference and date.
- Responsible licensed entity/person where required, certificate/licence validation and expiration.
- Insurance coverage, limits and exclusions reviewed.
- Contract responsibility matrix: who designs, installs, maintains, monitors, responds and warrants.
- Client permissions/privacy notices/retention rules as applicable.
- Review sign-off and revalidation date.

A licence for a partner does not automatically grant Blackpine authority to advertise or deliver regulated work directly.
