# Blackpine Assurance Evidence Register

Phase 5 baseline. This register controls when external-assurance claims may be published.

| Assurance item | Target/current reference | State | Minimum evidence before public claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 27001 | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 + Amd 1:2024 | OPEN | current certificate, scope, certification body, dates, certificate identifier/verification path, approved wording |
| SOC 2 Type II | AICPA Trust Services Criteria; Security baseline, Confidentiality where scoped | OPEN | final independent CPA report, report period, scoped system/services, applicable categories, controlled disclosure decision |
| ISO/IEC 27701 | ISO/IEC 27701:2025 | OPEN | current certificate, scope, certification body, dates, verification path, approved wording |
| ISO 22301 | ISO 22301:2019 + Amd 1:2024; successor edition under development in 2026 | OPEN | current certificate, applicable edition, scope, certification body, dates, verification path |
| ISO/IEC 20000-1 | ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 + Amd 1:2024 | OPEN | current certificate, scope, certification body, dates, verification path |
| Professional liability / E&O | organization-specific policy | OPEN | current certificate/policy evidence, insurer, coverage period, disclosure approval |
| Cyber liability | organization-specific policy | OPEN | current certificate/policy evidence, insurer, coverage period, disclosure approval |

## Claim rules

1. Readiness/alignment work is not certification.
2. An expired certificate cannot support a current-certification claim.
3. The published scope must not be broader than the certificate/report scope.
4. SOC 2 reports should normally be distributed through an appropriate controlled due-diligence process rather than posted publicly in full.
5. Logos/badges require the relevant issuer's permitted-use rules.
6. Evidence status stays OPEN until authoritative evidence is present and approved.
