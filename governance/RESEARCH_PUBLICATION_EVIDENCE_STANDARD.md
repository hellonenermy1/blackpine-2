# Research Publication Evidence Standard

## Purpose

Prevent Blackpine technical content from borrowing authority it has not earned. Publication labels must match the evidence behind the work.

## Publication classes

### Technical Guide
Operational guidance supported by authoritative or primary references where practical. A guide may contain Blackpine analysis, but it is not original research solely because Blackpine wrote it.

### Engineering Note
Focused analysis of a design, implementation, control or engineering problem. The note should identify the system boundary, assumptions and evidence available for inspection.

### Original Research
A publication based on a Blackpine-collected dataset, experiment, measurement campaign or other repeatable method. It must document the research question, method, dates, sample or environment, exclusions, limitations, analysis method and enough information to understand reproducibility.

### Security Advisory
A stable, revision-controlled notice about an affected condition that warrants action. CVE identifiers and vendor-response claims are included only when they actually exist.

### Tool Release
A Blackpine-owned utility or code release with purpose, version/revision context, limitations and safe-use boundaries.

## Minimum publication metadata

- title;
- publication class;
- publication date and material revision date where applicable;
- author or accountable owner where publication policy permits;
- defined question or purpose;
- evidence/source basis;
- limitations and scope boundary;
- source links or method description;
- correction/revision path.

## Original-research gate

A publication may be labelled Original Research only when all of the following exist:

1. a defined research question;
2. a Blackpine-collected dataset, experiment or measurement method;
3. collection dates and scope;
4. sample-selection or environment description;
5. exclusions and known sources of bias;
6. analysis method;
7. results supported by the recorded data;
8. limitations;
9. reproducibility information appropriate to the work;
10. confidentiality and disclosure review.

Source-backed commentary, a literature summary, a checklist, a security guide or a single product configuration review does not automatically meet this threshold.

## Vulnerability-research boundary

- Testing must be authorized.
- A submitted vulnerability report is not a CVE.
- A vendor response is not a public acknowledgement unless a publishable record exists.
- Do not publish exploit detail that creates unnecessary harm.
- Do not expose customer systems or confidential evidence.
- Preserve a disclosure timeline when coordinated disclosure becomes public.

## Corrections

Material errors should be corrected transparently. A correction should not silently preserve an obsolete conclusion when the evidence changed.
