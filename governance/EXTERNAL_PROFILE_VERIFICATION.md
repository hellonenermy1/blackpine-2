# External Profile Verification

## Purpose

Keep third-party company-profile links accurate and prevent similarly named organizations, stale listings, or unverified accounts from being presented as Blackpine.

## Verification steps

Before adding an external company profile to Blackpine's website:

1. Open the third-party record directly.
2. Confirm the displayed company name refers to Blackpine Cybersecurity.
3. Match stable public facts such as the approved business address, phone, website, or other authoritative identifiers.
4. Confirm the destination is controlled by the expected third-party platform and is not an intermediary, redirector, copied directory, or user-generated lookalike.
5. Confirm any badge, logo, rating, or review presentation complies with the platform's display rules.
6. Record the verification date and evidence status in `EXTERNAL_REPUTATION_REGISTER.md`.
7. Link only the verified destination. Do not guess usernames, company slugs, short URLs, or profile identifiers.
8. Re-check the record before promoting time-sensitive fields such as ratings, review counts, business hours, certifications, partnership status, or awards.
