# Blackpine Service Standards

Phase 5 baseline. Public commitments must match staffing, systems and contracts.

## Rules

- Business enquiries use the published contact path; no universal acknowledgement time is promised unless approved.
- Active-engagement communication, deliverables, dates, dependencies and escalation paths are governed by the applicable agreement/SOW.
- Material findings should be escalated through agreed engagement contacts when the engagement scope and staffing model support that path.
- Blackpine security incidents follow the documented incident-response lifecycle, including legal/contractual notification assessment.
- Public Trust Center material should satisfy routine due diligence first; sensitive evidence, insurance certificates and future audit reports use controlled disclosure.
- Do not publish 24/7 availability, guaranteed remediation time, uptime percentage, RTO, RPO or response SLA unless the commitment is contractually approved and operationally supported.

## Evidence required before publishing a measurable commitment

1. Named service owner.
2. Defined measurement method and start/stop conditions.
3. Staffing/escalation coverage matching the commitment.
4. Monitoring/reporting source.
5. Exception handling.
6. Contract/legal approval where applicable.
7. Operating evidence showing the target can be met.

Public marketing copy must never silently broaden a narrower contractual commitment.
