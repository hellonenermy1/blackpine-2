# Blackpine External Reputation Program

## Purpose

Build independently checkable reputation without manufacturing trust signals. External profiles, reviews, listings, partnerships, media records and technical acknowledgements are useful only when the underlying third-party record is genuine and current.

## Review principles

1. Ask only people or organizations with a genuine Blackpine business relationship or completed engagement experience.
2. Reviews are voluntary. Do not buy reviews, provide compensation for positive reviews, or make benefits conditional on leaving a review.
3. Do not review-gate by asking only clients expected to leave a positive rating while suppressing invitations to others based on anticipated sentiment.
4. Do not fabricate, duplicate, bulk-generate or impersonate customer reviews.
5. Do not give clients a pre-written review and ask them to post it as their own. A request may remind them of the engagement, but the review must remain in the client's own words.
6. Never ask a reviewer to expose confidential systems, findings, incident details, project values or other protected client information.
7. Employees, contractors, family members and other materially conflicted parties should not present themselves as independent customers.
8. Respond to public reviews professionally and factually without confirming confidential engagement details that the reviewer did not already make public and Blackpine is permitted to discuss.
9. Do not translate a review into a quantitative performance, ROI or risk-reduction claim unless separate evidence supports that claim.
10. Remove or correct Blackpine-owned references when the external record becomes stale, inaccurate or no longer available.

## Profile publication rule

Before a third-party profile is linked from the website:

- confirm the profile belongs to Blackpine;
- verify the public name, location and contact details against approved company facts;
- record the platform and verification date internally;
- verify any badge/logo use is permitted;
- avoid displaying dynamic ratings or counts unless there is a deliberate process to keep them current;
- do not infer profiles from similarly named companies or people.
