# Client Evidence Publication Standard

## Purpose

This standard governs testimonials, case studies, customer logos, outcomes, and other client-derived credibility evidence published by Blackpine.

## Evidence classes

### TESTIMONIAL
A statement attributed to a client contact. Publish only the wording and attribution approved for public use. If the client requests company confidentiality, do not infer or expose the company identity from project details.

### TESTIMONIAL-DERIVED SNAPSHOT
A short summary of an outcome explicitly supported by testimonial wording. It must identify the supporting testimonial internally and must not introduce environment size, percentages, savings, remediation rates, technical findings, or other facts not contained in approved evidence.

### ANONYMIZED CASE STUDY
A fuller engagement narrative requiring approved facts for, at minimum: sector or safe client description, problem, scope, material observations/findings, remediation, and result. A case study must not be created by filling missing fields with plausible generic details.

### NAMED CASE STUDY
An anonymized-case-study evidence package plus explicit approval to name the client organization and any people quoted.

## Publication rules

1. Preserve the substance of supplied testimonial wording.
2. Do not manufacture customer names, company names, logos, industries, locations, engagement values, dates, statistics, or outcomes.
3. Do not publish a customer logo without a verified right to do so.
4. Do not translate qualitative feedback into quantified risk reduction, ROI, cost savings, or percentage improvement without measurement evidence.
5. Do not describe a testimonial-derived snapshot as an independent case study.
6. If an attribution collides with a Blackpine employee identity or creates material ambiguity, withhold or clarify the public attribution until resolved.
7. Confidential engagement details remain confidential even when a testimonial is public.
8. Corrections or withdrawals requested by a client must be reflected promptly in the public material.

## Minimum case-study evidence packet

- client publication approval or documented publication basis;
- safe organization description;
- problem statement;
- agreed scope;
- material evidence/findings approved for publication;
- remediation or decision taken;
- outcome supported by evidence;
- testimonial wording if used;
- review owner and verification date.

## Current Blackpine Phase 4 boundary

The current supplied evidence supports 20 testimonials and three testimonial-derived snapshots. It does not, by itself, support three full anonymized case studies because the necessary scope, findings, remediation, and outcome details have not been supplied for publication.
